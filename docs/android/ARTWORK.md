# Artwork and branding

The launcher background, IV mark and app icon were supplied or selected by XHYN for this fork. The IV mark is used with a transparent background; the launcher uses the selected city artwork with a bottom gradient for readable menu controls.

Grand Theft Auto IV artwork and trademarks remain the property of their respective owners. Their inclusion as frontend branding does not place that artwork under the source code license. This project is unofficial and is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

No playable game, disc image or title update is included. Users must provide their own legally obtained game files.
